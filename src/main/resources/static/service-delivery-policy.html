Service Delivery Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026 • Techzyncmedia Digital Agency Services

🌐 Digital Service Notice: Techzyncmedia is an IT, Web Engineering, SEO, and Digital Marketing agency. All our deliverables are digital products, services, and cloud assets. No physical products are shipped or dispatched via courier.

1. Mode of Delivery

All services offered on our platform—including Web Development, Spring Boot APIs, SEO Audits, Performance Optimization, Digital Marketing campaigns, and Social Media Handling—are delivered electronically through secure digital channels, including:

  • Client Email & Digital Project Portals
  • Staging & Production Server Deployments (AWS, Cloud instances)
  • Git Code Repositories & Cloud Repositories
  • Shared Digital Drives & Analytics Dashboards

2. Service Kickoff & Fulfillment Timelines

Upon successful payment confirmation via Razorpay, service execution timelines operate as follows:

  • Immediate Order Confirmation: An automated digital payment receipt containing your Razorpay Payment Reference ID will be sent to your email immediately upon successful transaction completion.
  • Project Onboarding Kickoff: Our team will initiate communication and send an onboarding checklist or schedule a discovery call within 24 to 48 business hours of payment receipt.
  • Standard Service Delivery Windows:
    • Free Technical Audit / Analysis: Delivered within 24 hours.
    • SEO Setup & Audits: Initial audit and keyword roadmap delivered within 3–5 business days.
    • Social Media Management Retainers: Monthly content calendars and campaign plans delivered 5 days prior to month-start.
    • Custom Web & API Development: Milestone delivery schedule as per project scope (typically 1 to 4 weeks depending on complexity).

3. Delivery Confirmation & Client Acceptance

A service or deliverable is deemed delivered when access credentials, completed code repositories, live staging links, or audit documents are transmitted to the client's official email address. Clients have 5 business days to review digital deliverables and request minor adjustments as per project scope.

4. Delays & Dependencies

In cases where project execution depends on assets provided by the client (such as domain access, brand guidelines, content text, or third-party API keys), delivery timelines will be adjusted according to the date of receiving complete information.
